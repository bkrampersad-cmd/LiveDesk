# LiveDesk

Your desktop doesn't have to be a still photo. LiveDesk puts videos, animated GIFs and live, interactive scenes behind your desktop icons — an aquarium where the fish dart away from your mouse, fireworks you launch with a click, a sky that shows your real weather. It ships with 27 wallpapers ready to go, and it steps out of the way when you're working, pausing itself whenever an app is maximized or a game goes fullscreen.

## Features

- 🎞️ **Live Wallpapers** — any video, animated GIF or picture, looping smoothly behind your icons.
- 🖱️ **Interactive Wallpapers** — scenes that react to your mouse and clicks right on the desktop, or play on their own if you'd rather.
- 🌊 **27 Built In** — Aquarium, Fireworks, Lava Lamp, Northern Lights, Snow Globe, Ocean Waves, Live Sky (your real sunrise, sunset and weather), Fluids, Rain and more. No downloads needed.
- 🖥️ **Multi-Monitor** — a different wallpaper on each screen, the same one on all, or one stretched across them.
- 👀 **Preview & Customize** — try any wallpaper in a window first, and adjust its colors, speed and options live.
- ⏸️ **Smart Pausing** — pauses when an app or game covers the desktop, and optionally on battery, so it never slows you down.
- 🎛️ **Graphics Settings** — turn down the quality or frame rate for older or built-in graphics cards.
- 📦 **Lively Wallpaper Compatible** — bring over your Lively wallpapers in one click, or import Lively `.zip` packages.
- 📄 **Built-in User Manual** — a full PDF guide is bundled with every install.

## Requirements

- Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit)
